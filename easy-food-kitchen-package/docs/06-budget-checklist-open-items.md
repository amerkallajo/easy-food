## Phased checklist

Takeover is planned for the start of January 2027.

### Phase 0: validate (now to mid-December 2026)

- [ ] Footfall counts at four times of day, weekday and Saturday
- [ ] Photograph menus and prices at the ten nearest competitors
- [ ] Site survey: every item in "Critical measurements" below
- [ ] Ask the building owner and neighbours above about a facade exhaust
- [ ] Ask the municipality what licence a takeaway with cooking needs at this address, and what may stand on the sidewalk
- [ ] Ask civil defence about LPG cylinder storage for a shop with no outdoor area
- [ ] Recipe development and weighed costing for every launch item
- [ ] Two to three week pre-order pop-up from MarCoffee; record orders, repeat buyers and feedback
- [ ] Name checks: trademark agent, domain, Instagram and Facebook handles
- [ ] **Go / change / stop decision** against the rule in the feasibility assessment

### Phase 1: design and approvals (mid-December to mid-January)

- [ ] Update `build_model.py` with surveyed dimensions; regenerate plans
- [ ] Ventilation contractor: size hood, duct and fan; confirm route
- [ ] Electrician: load schedule, supply check, backup circuit list
- [ ] Gas fitter: locker, pipe route, detector and shut-off
- [ ] Three quotes each for stainless fabrication, refrigeration and the hood
- [ ] Submit licence and approval applications
- [ ] Open supplier accounts: chicken, beef, vegetables, bread, packaging

### Phase 2: build (January to February 2027, about 4-6 weeks)

- [ ] Strip out cafe fittings, false ceiling and wall cladding; re-check dimensions and the duct zone
- [ ] First-fix plumbing, drainage and floor drain; first-fix electrics
- [ ] Floor, coved skirting, wall finish, ceiling
- [ ] Shopfront: service window, apron, louvres, door overhaul
- [ ] Hood, duct, fan; gas locker and pipework; pressure test
- [ ] Install equipment in order: back table and sink, chillers, hot line, prep counter, packing bench (made to the measured gap), service counter
- [ ] Inverter and battery; air-conditioner
- [ ] Fascia, projecting sign, window and menu graphics

### Phase 3: commission and train (about 1-2 weeks)

- [ ] Commission the hood: smoke test at the griddle with the window sashes open and closed
- [ ] Gas leak test and detector test; fire equipment mounted
- [ ] Chillers hold below 5 °C for 48 hours, including through a simulated power cut on the battery
- [ ] Write the one-page procedures: opening, closing, cleaning, temperature log, raw-before-cooked rules, allergen list
- [ ] Staff training: three full practice services with timed tickets
- [ ] Photograph the final menu; publish ordering site, Google Business Profile, social pages
- [ ] Print calories and protein per item

### Phase 4: open

- [ ] Soft opening for one week: pop-up customers and nearby offices, limited hours
- [ ] Measure: orders per hour at peak, ticket time, waste, best sellers, repeat rate
- [ ] Adjust portions, prices and batch sizes
- [ ] Public opening; office meal cards; gym partnerships
- [ ] Monthly: re-cost recipes against market prices and the exchange rate
