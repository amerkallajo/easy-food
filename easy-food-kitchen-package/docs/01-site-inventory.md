## Conflicts and uncertainty

1. **Width: resolved at 2.79 m.** The brief said 2.60 m; the iPhone Measure screenshot reads 16'7" (5.06 m) and 9'2" (2.79 m), and the owner has confirmed 2.79 m. The design now uses 2.79 m, which gives a 1.39 m working aisle. Phone AR measurement is typically good to a few centimetres, so tape-check the width at the front, middle and back before any steel is fabricated: the right-hand line has no spare length.
2. **Where the measurement was taken.** The screenshot background shows light floor tiles and a curved timber counter front. The customer area has grey planks. The measurement was probably taken at or behind the bar.
3. **WC location resolved by the owner:** inside the rear-left corner; it must remain. The revised model reserves an assumed outer 1.20 × 1.60 m (1.92 m²). Its actual dimensions, walls and opening remain unmeasured. An accordion door was requested.
